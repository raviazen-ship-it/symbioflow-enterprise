# SYMBIOFLOW ENTERPRISE v4.0 - Design System & Direction

## Design Read
Reading this as: B2B Industrial Circular Economy Trading, 3D Digital Twin Material Inspector, Trade Finance Net-60 & Automated Scope-3 Carbon Ledger for factory procurement VPs, QC engineers, CFOs, and KLHK ESG auditors, in an Industrial Cyber-Ecology visual language, dial `ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1`.

## Aesthetic Anchor & Differentiator
- **Direction:** Industrial Cyber-Ecology Command Center (`#0B131E` / `#070D17` obsidian slate canvas with `#0F172A` instrumentation panels, `#10B981` circular verification signal, and `#22D3EE` technical Digital Twin / credit telemetry).
- **Signature Differentiator:** Synchronized Three.js WebGL Digital Twin (`granular`, `logistics`, `network`) coupled live to dry-basis weighbridge moisture QC math and tabular `JetBrains Mono` financial settlement tickers.

## Dial Settings
- **ENERGY: 2 (Balanced)** - Dense industrial telemetry and interactive 3D inspection without marketing theatrics.
- **RHYTHM: 2 (Consistent with purposeful breaks)** - Alternates between 7-step operational simulation, asymmetric 2-column marketplace + 3D laboratory workbench, reverse-auction board, 4-channel communication hub + live Deal Room, and financial/ESG calculation matrices.
- **MOTION: 1 (Hover, focus, and explicit user-triggered transitions only)** - No endless decorative loops or ambient bouncing; motion exists strictly on user-triggered 3D orbital inspection, exploded view (`1.85x`), AI COA scan progress, and active auction countdown seconds.

## One-Line Purpose Log (R-31)
- **Dark obsidian palette (`#0B131E` / `#0F172A`):** Maximizes contrast for 3D WebGL thermal/wireframe meshes and reduces eye strain in factory control rooms (R-21).
- **Emerald (`#10B981`/`#34D399`) & Cyan (`#06B6D4`/`#22D3EE`) accents:** Emerald signals verified KLHK circular compliance and escrow settlement; Cyan isolates AI COA matching, 3D shader controls, and Net-60 credit lines (R-01, R-29).
- **Amber (`#F59E0B`) & Rose (`#EF4444`/`#F87171`):** Amber warns of moisture penalties and tender deadlines; Rose highlights thermal moisture pockets in 3D X-Ray mode and validation errors.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans + JetBrains Mono (`tabular-nums`):** Humanist geometric sans keeps dense Indonesian corporate labels legible, while tabular monospace aligns Rupiah prices, metric tonnages, CAS registries, 13-digit NIBs, moisture percentages, SHA-256 hashes, and `tCO2e` figures vertically (R-06).
- **Asymmetric workbench layouts:** Pairs specification/catalog inputs on the left with real-time 3D WebGL or financial simulation outputs on the right so procurement decisions happen without tab-switching (R-05, R-14).
- **Industrial Lucide iconography:** Chosen per owner specification with domain-relevant glyphs (scale, factory, shield, truck, file-check, layers, flame) paired with high-contrast text labels (R-04).
